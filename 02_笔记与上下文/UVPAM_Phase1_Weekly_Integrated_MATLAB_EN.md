Week 1 starts **2026-09-07**. One experimental goal per week. A week is done when its **Done when** condition is demonstrated and recorded. MATLAB development starts from scratch and supports the experiments listed below.

- **Contact the histology core now.** Confirm sectioning onto our own 1.00 mm quartz slides, deparaffinization, and H&E staining of the same section after imaging. Open the billing account and arrange a pilot slide before Week 9.

**Notes**

- **Week 2 is a scope experiment.** Do not delay optical/acoustic alignment while waiting for the MATLAB acquisition program. For a 500 µm increase in acoustic path and an assumed sound speed of 1,480 m/s, the expected delay is approximately 338 ns; use the actual geometry and measurement uncertainty.
- **MATLAB supports the experiment.** Build only the functions needed for the current and next experimental step. Start with scripts and a small control interface; GUI polish does not determine whether an experimental milestone passes. Each new function needs mock-based `matlab.unittest` coverage; hardware tests are explicitly enabled.
- **Use hardware timing.** The free-running laser is referenced by conditioned PD pulses. NI counters generate STEP and count PD; the digitizer acquires records in batches. MATLAB loops, timers and UI callbacks must not provide pulse synchronization. The initial scan is step-and-settle; continuous motion needs a separate validated pulse-to-position scheme.
- **Keep the raw evidence.** Save raw data, coordinates, settings and acquisition status before generating images. Do not silently fill missing data. Stop STEP output first on faults, then abort acquisition and preserve available data. Software does not replace independent safety layers.
- **Check speed early, in Week 4.** At 1 µm pitch, a 3 mm × 3 mm field contains approximately one million pixels: the 30-minute target allows less than 1.8 ms per pixel once frame overhead is included. At 0.5 µm pitch, it allows less than 0.45 ms. Pixel pitch and measured optical resolution are separate quantities. If the measured budget fails, record the required change in field, time or architecture before Week 10.
- **Qualify the chosen PRF.** Start at 100 or 200 kHz; higher rates up to 2 MHz require separate checks of trigger timing, record window, acoustic overlap and throughput. Count illumination during motion/settling/idle time when assessing exposure; disabling acquisition does not stop the laser.
- **Protect the three buffer weeks.** First light, hardware integration and tissue preparation may slip. If a Friday acceptance condition is unmet, record the blocker and finish it before starting dependent experiments; independent preparation can continue.
